RE4 Zeebo Edition

Experience a rare and distinctive version of the survival horror classic — Resident Evil 4: Zeebo Edition, ported from the exclusive Zeebo console directly onto PlayStation Portable hardware! Built on the BREW and OpenGL ES 1.1 architecture, this edition stands out with its unique visual atmosphere, custom level balancing, and the iconic blue-skinned Los Ganados villagers. This native PSP port features full analog nub and button controls, smooth performance, and complete localization support. Step into the boots of special agent Leon S. Kennedy, fight through a sinister rural village, rescue the President's daughter Ashley Graham, and uncover the dark secret of the Las Plagas parasite right on your handheld.
