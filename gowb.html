God of War: Betrayal

Step into the sandals of the new God of War in the canonical chapter of the Kratos saga — God of War: Betrayal, ported natively onto PlayStation Portable hardware! Set between Ghost of Sparta and God of War II, the story follows the Ghost of Sparta leading the Spartan army on a rampage across Greece, only to be framed for the murder of the giant beast Argos in a sinister plot to turn Olympus against him. Featuring responsive native PSP button controls, brutal combos with the Blades of Athena, the mighty Blade of Olympus, godly magic, and clashes with mythical monsters, this handheld port delivers classic Spartan vengeance anywhere you go.
